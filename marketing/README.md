Every asset points to the same two destinations and tells the same honest story.

- **Live landing page:** https://gnaidu05.github.io/WWH/
- **Repo:** https://github.com/gnaidu05/WWH

- **The honest number:** **85** handwritten agents across **16** divisions — *start
  with 7, scale to 85.* (Not 232. No inflated stars, testimonials, or metrics in any asset.)

## The launch, in one paragraph

Organic-first. The engine is a strong **Show HN** and a share in the right
Claude/AI-coding communities, pointed at the live landing page, backed by a
launch-day **X thread** and **LinkedIn post**. The **"Starter Squad cheat
sheet"** is the lead magnet that turns visitors into an email list the
onboarding sequence then activates (install → first agent → the orchestrator →
make it yours). SEO and paid are amplification, not the launch itself.

## Definition of done (the reality-checker's bar)

- Every asset uses the real numbers and the real URLs.
- No claimed traction that doesn't exist yet — targets are labeled as targets.
- Every asset has exactly one clear CTA to the site or the repo.
