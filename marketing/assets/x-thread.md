**1/**

**2/**
A generic prompt has no spine.

It doesn't know if it owes you a schema, a test plan, a landing page, or a threat model — so it gives you a little of everything and commits to nothing.

That's not a specialist. That's a stranger with confidence.

**3/**
The Agency is an open-source library of 85 handwritten AI specialist agents.

MIT licensed. Free. Installs into the tool you already use — Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex, Gemini, Windsurf, Aider — with one command:

`scripts/install.sh`

**4/**
Every agent pins down 4 things a generic prompt leaves blank:

• who it is
• what it specializes in
• how it actually works
• what it owes you back — a concrete deliverable

A prompt is a suggestion. An agent is a contract.

**5/**
"Act as a developer" → a stranger who might do anything.

`backend-architect` → knows it owns the data model and service boundaries, works them in order, and hands back an actual design.

Not three paragraphs on why architecture matters.

**6/**

Engineering · Design · Product · Marketing · Sales · Security · Finance · Testing/QA · Game Dev · Data/AI · DevOps · Operations · Research · Mapping/GIS · Content · Growth

**7/**
