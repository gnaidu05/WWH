# LinkedIn Launch Post — The Agency

**Audience:** technical founders, builders, and engineers in a professional feed.
**Goal:** awareness → consideration; frame The Agency as a team you assemble, not another prompt pack.
**CTA:** browse the roster + install the starter squad.

---

Every founder I know has typed some version of "act as a senior engineer" into their AI tool and hoped for the best.

The model then fills in everything you didn't say — who that person is, how they work, what they're supposed to deliver — with a plausible average. You get confidence without a spine. A little of everything, a commitment to nothing.

So we tried something less clever and more useful: we wrote the specialists down by hand. 85 of them.

**The Agency** is an open-source library of 85 handwritten AI specialist agents. It's MIT licensed, it's free, and it installs into the AI coding tool your team already uses — Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex, Gemini, Windsurf, Aider — with a single command.

What makes an "agent" different from a prompt is that each one is a contract, not a suggestion. Every agent pins down four things:

• Who it is — a real role with a point of view
• What it specializes in — and what it deliberately doesn't
• How it works — an actual workflow
• What it owes you back — a concrete deliverable you can open and use

The 85 span 16 divisions — Engineering, Design, Product, Marketing, Sales, Security, Finance, Testing & QA, Game Dev, Data & AI, DevOps & Infra, Operations, Research, Mapping & GIS, Content & Editorial, and Growth. It's shaped like a company, not a toolbox.

Which is why the most important instruction is: don't install all 85. Nobody onboards a whole company on a Tuesday. Start with a 7-agent squad — rapid-prototyper, backend-architect, ai-engineer, whimsy-injector, growth-hacker, content-creator, reality-checker — and add the rest when you have a real job for them.

The part that makes it feel like a team rather than a folder of prompts is the orchestrator. You hand the `agents-orchestrator` a goal, and it runs the specialists toward it — one builds, one hardens the backend, one finds the growth loop, one writes it up, one calls out what isn't actually true yet. You direct; the company works.

And because it's open source, it's yours. Not a SaaS you rent — a library you clone, read, and edit. Every agent is a plain file you can rewrite in your own voice. A starting point you own.
