# The Unspoken Bloom — 30-Second Product Spot
**Format:** 30.0s, 16:9 (3840×2160 master), 24fps
**Hero object:** the physical book (cover art as supplied)
**Emotional beat (the one idea):** the cost of the unsaid word — *say it before it's too late.*
**Voiceover:** none. An ad about the unspoken stays silent; on-screen type and music carry the whole arc.
**Backdrop:** soft warm white / cream (`#F4EDE2`), pulled from the cover's own field. The cover is light and airy — it breathes on cream where it would look pasted on black.
**Palette (from cover, kept tight):** cream `#F4EDE2` · wheat `#C9A97A` · sky grey-blue `#B9C4C9` · ink charcoal `#1E1E1E`
**Typography:** one crisp neo-grotesque sans (Inter / Helvetica-class), ink charcoal, wide tracking (+8%), always lowercase-calm sentence case, never more than one line of type on screen at a time.
**Sound:** felt piano, single sparse notes over faint room tone. No percussion. One gentle swell at the reveal. Silence is scored deliberately — it *is* the theme.
**End card:** title, author, tagline only. (No publisher was supplied, so none appears.)

## 2. Shot-by-shot storyboard

**Shot 1 — "The empty room" — 0:00.0–0:03.5 (3.5s)**
- **Framing:** Empty cream field, 16:9. Nothing in frame. Subtle film grain; light very gently "breathes" (±2% exposure over the shot).
- **Camera:** Locked off.
- **Text:** "They never said it." — fades in over 0.8s, dead center, small (≈2% frame height).
- **Sound:** Room tone; one felt-piano note.
- **Intent:** Open on absence. The viewer leans in.

**Shot 2 — "Paper" — 0:03.5–0:07.5 (4.0s)**
- **Framing:** Extreme macro of the cover as a printed object: the wheat-field grain of the artwork and the paper texture, occupying the lower third; the rest is soft cream bokeh.
- **Camera:** Very slow lateral drift, left to right (≈3% of frame width over the shot). Shallow depth of field.
- **Text:** "Not when it was easy." — lower-left third, clear of the detail.
- **Sound:** Second piano note, lower.
- **Intent:** The book as a physical, premium object — Apple's macro-on-aluminum move, on paper.

**Shot 3 — "The embrace" — 0:07.5–0:11.5 (4.0s)**
- **Framing:** Macro on the cover's couple — the embrace — starting out of focus, resolving to sharp over the full shot. Framed off-center right, generous negative space left.
- **Camera:** Focus pull only; body locked.
- **Text:** "Not when it mattered." — in the negative space, left.
- **Sound:** Third note, then held silence.
- **Intent:** First glimpse of the heart of the cover, earned slowly.

**Shot 4 — "In shadow" — 0:11.5–0:16.5 (5.0s)**
- **Framing:** Wide. The book standing upright, small in frame (≈20% of frame height), right-of-center on the cream sweep, in soft deep shadow — cover barely legible. Vast negative space.
- **Camera:** Imperceptible push-in (≈4% over the shot) as a soft key light slowly rises across the cover, left to right, like dawn.
- **Text:** None. This is the held-breath beat.
- **Sound:** Warm low drone enters under the piano; the score "opens."
- **Intent:** The turn of the arc — from what was never said toward what's about to be. Light = speaking.

**Shot 5 — "The reveal" — 0:16.5–0:23.0 (6.5s) — continuous with Shot 4 if rendered as one take**
- **Framing:** The book now fully lit, centered, ≈55% of frame height. Full cover crisply legible for the first time. Soft contact shadow beneath; nothing else in frame.
- **Camera:** Slow settle: the push-in eases to a stop (Apple's "arrive and hold").
- **Text:** "Some love stories grow in silence…" fades in at 0:19.0, centered beneath the book.
- **Sound:** The single swell of the spot; piano turns melodic.
- **Intent:** The hero moment. The tagline lands only once the object has fully arrived.

**Shot 6 — "The name" — 0:23.0–0:27.0 (4.0s)**
- **Framing:** Same composition, breathing room preserved. Tagline dissolves out; title block fades in beneath the book: "The Unspoken Bloom" with "a novel by Gopinathan Naidu" smaller below.
- **Camera:** Locked. Total stillness.
- **Text:** Title + author.
- **Sound:** Piano resolves; drone fades out.
- **Intent:** Confidence. The product states its name once, quietly.

**Shot 7 — "End card" — 0:27.0–0:30.0 (3.0s)**
- **Framing:** Clean end card: cream field, the cover small and centered (≈30% frame height), type block beneath — title, author, tagline in one quiet stack.
- **Camera:** Locked.
- **Text:** "The Unspoken Bloom · a novel by Gopinathan Naidu · Some love stories grow in silence…"
- **Sound:** Scored silence; one final note decays past the cut to black at 0:30.0.
- **Intent:** Leave the room the way we entered it — quiet, but changed.

- Cover fidelity is non-negotiable: all shots featuring legible cover art must be driven by the supplied cover image (image-to-video), never re-imagined by the model. Type is composited in post, not model-generated.
- Music generated to brief above (felt piano, ~66 BPM feel, no percussion, 30s with tail).
- No voiceover to record.
