Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitaab ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our marketplace for Indian authors, publishers, distributors, reviewers and readers.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the marketplace:

  • Account information — your name, email address and password (passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text).
  • Profile information — the role, city, language, organisation, bio and profile picture you choose to add.
  • Content you create — books you list (title, ISBN, price, cover image, marketplace links), events you host, and messages you send us.
  • Basic usage data — a light-touch theme preference stored in your browser (light/dark). We do not run advertising trackers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To display your listings, events and author profile to other users, as you intend.
  • To let publishers, distributors, reviewers, authors and readers discover and contact one another.
  • To respond to messages and provide support.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse.

3. What is public

Kitaab is a marketplace, so some information is intentionally public: your author/partner profile (name, role, bio, picture), your book listings, and your events are visible to other visitors. Your email address, password and city are never shown publicly. Please only add information you're comfortable making public.

4. How your data is stored

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL platform) with row-level security so that only you can edit your own profile, books and events. Uploaded images are held in access-controlled storage.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the infrastructure providers that operate the service (such as our database/storage host and our website host), and where required by law.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile and listings at any time from your dashboard, and delete individual books and events. To delete your account and associated data entirely, or to request a copy of your data, contact us.

7. Children

Kitaab is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us through our Contact page.
