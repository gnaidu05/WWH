Our mission

Every Indian story, one marketplace

India's book world is thriving — but scattered. Kitaab brings it together in a single, welcoming home.

Today, an Indian author has to juggle a dozen disconnected tools: one platform to publish, another to sell, a spreadsheet of reviewers, WhatsApp groups for events, and cold emails to distributors. Readers, meanwhile, struggle to discover the incredible range of writing being produced in 22 languages across the country.

Kitaab changes that. It's one marketplace where authors list their books and host engagement activities; where publishers, distributors and reviewers share their quotations openly; and where readers discover, buy and celebrate Indian writing — all in one place.

Our name, Kitaab (किताब), means "the book" — the vessel that carries a story from one mind to another, across languages and generations. We're building the marketplace those books deserve.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and setting up your author page is free. You only pay for optional services you choose from partners.

Can I sell books in regional languages?

Absolutely. Kitaab supports 22 Indian languages, and readers can filter by language to find your work.

How do partner quotations work?

Publishers, distributors and reviewers post their packages and prices. Authors browse, compare and request a quote directly — no cold outreach.

What kinds of events can I host?

Book launches, readings, author AMAs, workshops, panels and book clubs — online or in-person, and you announce them to the whole community.
