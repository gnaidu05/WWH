# Niche Research — Faceless YouTube Channel (July 2026)

Research date: 2026-07-12. Data sources: vidIQ breakout-channel index, vidIQ keyword
research, and public niche/RPM roundups (linked at the bottom).

## Selected niche: **AI Tools & Tips** (Top-X ranking Shorts + tutorials)

Format: faceless 45–60s vertical Shorts — "Top 3 AI tools for X", "This new AI tool
does Y", "Stop using X, use this instead" — built from stock b-roll, AI voiceover,
and bold text overlays. Long-form tutorials can be layered in once the channel has
traction.

## Why this niche won

Signal: head terms have massive volume with only moderate competition, and
*tool-specific tutorial* keywords are wide open (competition ~20/100). Coverage of
newly launched tools ("nano banana pro") spikes to six-figure searches within weeks —
a repeatable trend-jacking play.

### 2. Format signal from fastest-growing faceless channels (vidIQ breakout index)

Sampling the top faceless breakout channels (EN, >10k subs, sorted by 30-day
subscriber growth) shows the growth is concentrated in **format-driven Shorts**:

- Ranking/"Top X" Shorts of trending topics — e.g. a rankings channel at **865k subs,
  +488% subs/30d, ~2.9M avg views** per video
- Pop-culture commentary Shorts — 153k subs, +493%/30d
- Compilations / "definitely top 5" humor — +478%/30d
- High-earning faceless *long-form* outliers: miniature engineering builds
  (~$4.1k/mo est.), music mixes (~$1.2k/mo est.), 3D animation loops (~$3.3k/mo est.)

The **Top-X ranking format** is the transferable, automatable pattern.

### 3. Monetization (public RPM data, July 2026 roundups)

- AI/tech education is repeatedly cited as the fastest-growing faceless category of
  2026 with RPMs of **$15+** (vs $6–13 for horror narration / ASMR / soundscapes).
- Adjacent finance formats reach $18–45 RPM if the channel later expands into
  "AI for investing/business" content.
- Strong affiliate layer: AI SaaS affiliate programs pay recurring commissions,
  which Shorts descriptions can carry from day one.

### 4. Automation fit

Every asset in this niche is machine-generatable: scripts (LLM), voiceover (neural
TTS), visuals (stock b-roll + screen-style text overlays), and topics (RSS feeds of
AI-news sites + an evergreen queue). No gameplay capture, no licensed clips, no
fair-use gray zones.

## Runner-up niches considered

1. **Ranking Shorts of trending pop culture** — fastest raw growth, but depends on
   third-party clips (fair-use risk) and low RPM.
2. **Ambient/music mixes** — highest watch time and strong est. earnings, but AI
   music generation at daily volume is the most expensive asset to produce, and
   YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy update has hit low-effort music
   loops hardest.
3. **Miniature/DIY engineering** — great earnings but not automatable (real footage).

## Compliance note (important)

YouTube's monetization policy (updated July 2025) demonetizes "mass-produced,
repetitious, inauthentic" content. This pipeline is designed to stay on the right
side of that line: every video has a unique researched script, topical relevance,
and human-reviewable output. Keep a human in the loop on scripts/uploads early on,
and add original insight (rankings with reasoning, comparisons, benchmarks) rather
than pure text-to-speech regurgitation.
