## What you can do on Kitaab

### 📖 List your book and reach real readers
Create a listing in minutes — cover, blurb, genre, language, price, and direct
buy links to Amazon, Flipkart, or wherever you sell. Every book gets its own
page and shows up in the marketplace next to India's newest writing, in every
language.

### 🤝 Line up your publishing team *while you're still writing*
This is the part most platforms miss. From your dashboard you can browse and
search **publishers, distributors, and reviewers** — see their packages and
quotations, and request a quote — *in parallel* with your writing. No waiting
until the last page to start figuring out editing, design, ISBN, printing, or
distribution. Your team can be ready when your manuscript is.

### 🎤 Host and announce engagement activities
Launches, readings, AMAs, workshops, panels, book clubs — online or in person.
Announce your event, add a venue or a join link, and let readers RSVP
(Coming / Maybe / Interested) so you know who's showing up.

### ⭐ Earn reviews and ratings that build trust
Readers can rate and review your books. Honest, visible reviews help new readers
discover you and give your next launch real social proof.

### 🌟 Get your own public author page
A shareable profile with your bio, your books, and your events — one link you can
put in your social bios, email signature, and press kit.
